Project Vision & Goals

The Three Critical Campus Library Challenges

🧭 1. Physical Lost Time

  • Students spend 15–25 minutes wandering between library stacks trying to locate books.
  • Outdated OPAC catalogs only show Dewey decimal numbers without spatial floor or rack maps.
  • Results in frustration, abandoned searches, and empty-shelf disappointments.
Solved with 4-Tier Spatial Coordinate Index

🪑 2. Study Space Friction

  • Severe peak-season study crowding and lack of real-time seat availability glance.
  • Concurrence booking clashes and disputes over silent pods and team rooms.
  • No transparency on floor noise zones (Silent Carrel vs. Collaborative Lab).
Solved with Atomic Seat Map & Slot Locks

🔍 3. Outdated Discovery

  • Traditional keyword search fails when students remember concepts rather than exact titles.
  • Zero personalized recommendations aligned with individual student research interests.
  • Lack of trusted, verified chat assistants grounded in real library availability.
Solved with TF-IDF Vector Matcher & Chatbot

Natural Language Processing

AI Book Finder & Semantic Vector Matching

🧠 In-Memory TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity

  • Query Pre-Processing: Cleans punctuation, removes stop-words, and stems research terms.
  • Vector Representation: Indexes catalog titles, authors, categories, and deep technical summaries into dimensional term vectors.
  • Cosine Similarity Calculation: Determines semantic proximity between student query vectors and book profile vectors in under 15ms.
  • Explainable AI: Returns transparent match reasons (e.g., "Matches relevant topic terms: [clean, code, software] in Software Engineering.")

🤖 Personalized Recommendations & Chatbot

  • Academic Profile Onboarding: Captures student majors and research interests during account registration.
  • Content-Based Suggestion Engine: Continuously computes affinity scores against borrowing history to surface curated reading carousels.
  • LibraMind AI Assistant: Grounded conversational bot strictly referencing active catalog records to provide verified floor locations and live copy availability without hallucination.
Physical Retrieval Optimization

3D Shelf Coordinates & Visit Planner

📍 4-Tier Spatial Indexing

  • Floor Level: Floor 1 (General & Tech), Floor 2 (Humanities & Labs), Floor 3 (Archives).
  • Section: Tech & Engineering, Computing, Literature, etc.
  • Shelf Code: Explicit alphanumeric aisle identifier (e.g., Shelf SE-04).
  • Rack Number: Precision vertical rack bay (e.g., Rack 03).

🏷️ Copy-Level Barcode Tracking

  • Each book tracks individual physical copies (e.g., LM-SE-04-C1, LM-SE-04-C2).
  • Live status indicators: available, borrowed, maintenance, reserved.
  • Physical condition tracking: Excellent, Good, Fair.
  • Clear loan status prevents empty-handed shelf visits.

🗺️ My Library Visit Planner

  • Add desired books into an active personalized visit itinerary.
  • Automatically groups books by Floor and Section.
  • Displays real-time study seat density for each destination floor.
  • Interactive checklist allowing students to mark items as found during physical rounds.
Study Space Discovery & Concurrency

Interactive Study Pods & Anti-Conflict Engine

🪑 40 Dedicated Pods Across 3 Zones

  • Silent Focus Pods: Sound-insulated carrels with high-speed power ports.
  • Collaborative Labs: Multi-seat team tables equipped with HDMI docks and whiteboards.
  • Window Nooks: Biophilic daylight spaces featuring ergonomic lounge seating.
  • Smart Filters: Instant filtering by floor, zone environment, and equipment (Power, Monitor, Whiteboard).

🔒 Database-Enforced Concurrency Safety

  • Strict SQLite Partial Unique Index ensures double-booking is physically impossible:
    • CREATE UNIQUE INDEX idx_seat_active_reservation
    ON seat_reservations (seat_id, date, time_slot)
    WHERE status = 'active';
  • Simultaneous reservation attempts by competing students are handled atomically—one gets reserved, the other receives an instant friendly conflict message.
  • Librarian controls allow immediate maintenance lockout toggles.
Desk Operations & Queueing

Circulation Rules & Ordered Hold Queues

📦 Lending Policies & Automated Fines

  • Standard Loan Term: 14 days with real-time due date countdown indicator.
  • Automated Fines: $1.00/day fine accrued automatically on overdue items.
  • Self-Service Renewals: Up to 2 loan renewals permitted when no hold queue exists.
  • Fast Desk Checkout: Instant barcode scanning for physical book issue & return.

👥 Ordered FIFO Waitlist Hand-off

  • When 100% of copies are checked out, patrons join an ordered hold queue.
  • Transparent sequence feedback: "You are #1 of 3 in queue."
  • Automated Hand-off: Upon book return, the copy transitions to 'reserved' exclusively for Queue #1 with an alert notification.
  • 48-hour pickup window before cascading to Queue #2.
